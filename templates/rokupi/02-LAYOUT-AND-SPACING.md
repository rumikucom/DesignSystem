# Layout & Spacing — Rokupi

> Page structure, grid spacing, section spacing, and container system.

---

## Clear Space & Configuration

The Rokupi brand relies on proper spacing to maintain a clean, proportional look.

### Logo Clear Space
- **Minimum Clear Area:** A clear area with the correct application of the logo will make the logo proportion in a design look proportional and maintain visual integrity. Do not crowd the logo with other elements or text.

### Minimum Size
- **Primary Logo:** Do not scale the Primary Logo below **20mm** in height.
- **Secondary Logo:** If the space requires a logo height of less than 20mm (or a very wide aspect ratio), use the Secondary Logo.
- **Proportions:** The scale and proportion of the logo must be determined by available space, aesthetics, function, and visibility. **DO NOT stretch or rotate the logo.**

---

## Alignment

### Text Alignment
- **Strictly Left-Aligned:** All body text must be left-aligned. This provides readers with a constant starting point for each line, drastically improving legibility and readability.
- **Avoid Centered/Justified Body Text:** Do not use justified or centered alignment for paragraphs of text.

---

## Page Shell & Containers

Rokupi's layout should be straightforward and functional, mirroring its physical store presence (signboards, facades).

- **Container Width:** Standard centered containers (e.g., `1024px` or `1200px` max-width) to ensure text lines do not become unreadably long.
- **Padding:** Generous padding around sections to emulate the "Clear Space" concept applied to the overall layout, letting the bold primary colors and rounded typography breathe.
