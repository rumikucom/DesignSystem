# Rokupi Design Preset

> Design preset for the Rokupi brand. A vibrant, friendly design system defined by its bold Blue and Pink primary colors, rounded typography (Fredoka), and clean, legible layout.

---

## Purpose

This preset captures the visual language of Rokupi, which utilizes highly legible typography and strong primary contrast. It features a straightforward color palette centered on high-impact primary elements with friendly, rounded aesthetics.

### Key Design Characteristics

- **Friendly Typography**: Exclusive use of the Google Font `Fredoka` for a rounded, approachable feel.
- **Bold Primary Colors**: Intense Blue (#0123FF) and vibrant Pink/Red (#F40362).
- **High Contrast Backgrounds**: Clean White (#FFFFFF) and solid Black (#000000) backgrounds for maximum readability.
- **Left-Aligned Text**: All body text is strictly left-aligned for consistent reading flow.
- **Calculated Line Height**: Auto-leading formula for excellent legibility (Line height ≈ 1.4).

### What This Preset Defines

- The core color palette (Blue, Pink, Black, White).
- Typography scale and font configuration for Fredoka.
- Layout patterns ensuring proper clear space and readability.
- Component styling suitable for physical and digital brand expression.

## How to Use

1. **Apply the Color Palette**: Use Blue and Pink strategically on White or Black backgrounds to maximize contrast.
2. **Use Fredoka**: Use Fredoka Semi Bold for all headlines and Fredoka Regular for body text.
3. **Follow Layout Rules**: Ensure text is left-aligned and logos have adequate clear space as defined in `02-LAYOUT-AND-SPACING.md`.
