# Brand Core Identity — Rokupi

> Core brand identity for the Rokupi preset.

## Brand Basics

- **Brand Name:** Rokupi
- **Logo:** (Consists of Icon and Typeface)
- **Core Mission/Tagline:** Friendly, legible, and simple retail/F&B brand.
- **Description:** Rokupi is a brand with a strong visual identity centered around bold primary colors (Blue and Pink) and friendly, rounded typography. The brand expression spans digital media, physical signboards, facades, and products.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Color:** `#0123FF` (Vibrant Blue — RGB: 1, 35, 255)
- **Secondary Color:** `#F40362` (Vibrant Pink/Red — RGB: 244, 3, 98)
- **Neutral Base (Light):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
- **Neutral Base (Dark):** `#000000` (Black)

## Typography Selections

- **Display/Heading Font:** Fredoka (Semi Bold)
- **Body/Sans Font:** Fredoka (Regular)
- **Available Weights:** Light, Regular, Medium, Semi Bold, Bold

## Design Principles

- **Clear Space:** Maintain minimum clear area around the logo to ensure proportional and clean design.
- **Minimum Size:** Logo height must be at least 20mm. For smaller sizes, use the Secondary Logo.
- **Contrast:** Ensure high readability by using appropriate logo colors depending on the background (e.g., white logo on black/dark backgrounds, colored logo on white/light backgrounds).
- **Text Alignment:** Text is always left-aligned to provide a constant starting point for reading.
